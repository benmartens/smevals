## Summary

Implemented `CartonPacker.Pack` (`src/CartonPacking/CartonPacker.cs`) — deterministic, dependency-free.

**Algorithm**
1. **Normalize** — drop malformed/duplicate carton types.
2. **Selection (value-first)** — if total weight fits, take everything; otherwise a **bounded knapsack** over container weight maximizes total value, then total volume (weight‑0 cartons always included). This defeats the greedy weight/value trap (2 light > 1 heavy).
3. **Placement** — constructive *drop-to-lowest-fully-supported-surface*: for each carton, every orientation × back-left candidate coordinate is tested; a slot is valid only when the footprint rests on a perfectly flat, fully-covered surface (floor or coplanar carton tops), which guarantees 100% base support and zero overlap. The lowest, most back-left slot wins deterministically.
4. **Space fill** — remaining cartons are seated where weight and space still allow, recovering value/volume.
5. Output sorted by carton ID, instance, X, Y, Z.

Handles rotation, `keepUpright`, shared support, quantity, and weight limits.
