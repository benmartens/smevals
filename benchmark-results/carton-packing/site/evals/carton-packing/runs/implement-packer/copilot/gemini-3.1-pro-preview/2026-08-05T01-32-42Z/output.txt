I have successfully implemented `CartonPacker.Pack`. I used a Depth-First Search (DFS) algorithm to explore possible carton placements.

Here is a summary of the algorithm:
*   **State Representation**: The state tracks current placements, remaining carton quantities, accumulated weight, value, volume, and the coordinates of the last evaluated point.
*   **Point Generation**: Candidate points for placement are generated from the extrema (X+Width, Y+Depth, Z+Height) of already placed cartons, plus the origin (0,0,0).
*   **Move Validation**: At each point, it checks all orientations of remaining cartons that fit within the container bounds, don't exceed the max weight limit, don't overlap with existing cartons, and have 100% base support if placed above the floor (`Z > 0`).
*   **Heuristic Ordering**: Moves are evaluated and sorted based on a heuristic that prioritizes higher value relative to volume and weight consumed, followed by volume and dimension sizes. This greedy ordering guides the DFS towards better solutions early.
*   **Search**: The DFS explores placing a valid carton at a candidate point or skipping the point. It prunes the search if a perfect packing (all cartons placed) is found or if a maximum node limit (100,000) is reached to prevent excessive runtime.
*   **Result**: The best state encountered (maximizing value, then volume) is tracked and its sorted placements are returned as the result.
