### Summary of Algorithm and Testing

#### Algorithm
`CartonPacker.Pack` implements a deterministic, multi-strategy 3D packing engine designed to maximize total shipment value and packed volume under container weight, volume, rotation, orientation (`keepUpright`), and full-base support constraints:

1. **Exact Bounded Depth-First Search (DFS)**: For smaller problem instances ($\le 16$ instances), runs exact DFS with upper-bound pruning and canonical state exploration to find mathematically optimal placements.
2. **Multi-Start Priority & Block Packing Heuristics**: Generates candidate placement positions via corner/boundary extreme point projections. Evaluates carton sort metrics (value/weight ratio, value/volume ratio, footprint area, surface contact score) and macro block building ($k_x \times k_y \times k_z$) with deterministic pseudo-random GRASP passes.
3. **Iterated Greedy Local Search**: Performs post-optimization by selectively removing low-density items and re-packing remaining space with higher-value unpacked cartons.
4. **Validation & Canonical Sorting**: Reuses `PackingValidator.HasFullBaseSupport` and `PackingValidator.Overlaps` to guarantee valid 3D placements, sorting final results by `CartonId` (ordinal), `Instance`, `X`, `Y`, and `Z`.
