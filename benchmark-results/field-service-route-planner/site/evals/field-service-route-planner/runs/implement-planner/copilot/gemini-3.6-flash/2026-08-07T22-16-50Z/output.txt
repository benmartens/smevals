### Summary of Algorithm & Tests

#### Algorithm
`RoutePlanner.Plan` implements a deterministic strategy to lexicographically maximize served job value and minimize total travel time:
1. **Canonical Indexing:** Technicians are sorted by ID (`StringComparer.Ordinal`) to guarantee canonical output order. Locations and skills are indexed for $O(1)$ matrix and capability checks.
2. **Branch & Bound Solver ($N \le 64$):** Evaluates exact permutations using bitmask state tracking, Pareto dominance memoization `(time, travel)`, upper-bound value pruning, and consecutive identical technician symmetry breaking.
3. **Metaheuristic Fallback & Refinement:** For larger problems or timeouts, ALNS + Variable Neighborhood Local Search (2-Opt, Relocate, Swap, Unassigned Insertion/Replacement) optimizes routes deterministically (`Random(42)`).
