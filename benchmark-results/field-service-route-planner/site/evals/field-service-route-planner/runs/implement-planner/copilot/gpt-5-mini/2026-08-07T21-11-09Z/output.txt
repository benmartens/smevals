Implemented RoutePlanner.Plan using per-technician subset DP and global DP to assign disjoint job subsets. Algorithm:
- Enumerate jobs and technicians deterministically.
- For each technician, DP over feasible local job permutations (respecting windows, waiting, returns, shifts) to find, per subset, the best service order with minimal travel.
- Global DP combines per-technician subset choices to lexicographically maximize total served value, then minimize total travel.
- Deterministic iteration and tie-breakers ensure repeatable output.
