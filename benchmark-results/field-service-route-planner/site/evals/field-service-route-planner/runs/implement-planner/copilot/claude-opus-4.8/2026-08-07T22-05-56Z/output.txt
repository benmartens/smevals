## Summary

**Implementation** (`src/FieldServiceRoutePlanner/RoutePlanner.cs`): a deterministic exact solver with a safe fallback.

1. **Per-technician Held-Karp DP** over subsets of skill-compatible jobs. Each `(subset, lastJob)` state keeps a **Pareto frontier of (completion-time, travel)** pairs, capturing waiting, time windows, and asymmetric directed travel. This yields the minimum directed travel (including the depot return) for every feasible job subset, respecting shift start/end.
2. **Subset-DP combine** across technicians (sorted by ID). Since a used-job bitmask fully determines served value, keeping min-travel per mask is lossless; the final pick is **lexicographic: max total value, then min total travel** over disjoint assignments.
3. **Deterministic reconstruction**: DFS in ascending job-ID order recovers the lexicographically smallest optimal ordering hitting the target travel.
4. **Guards + greedy fallback**: budget limits trigger a deterministic greedy insertion heuristic that still returns a fully valid plan. Every technician appears exactly once in canonical order.
