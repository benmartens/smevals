### Algorithm
Hybrid deterministic solver in `RoutePlanner.cs`:
1. **Multi-start greedy insertion** — several job orderings (value, window, tightness, location, density) plus sequential NN build  
2. **Local search** — relocate, swap, 2-opt, cross-exchange (max value, then min travel)  
3. **Exact DFS** (≤12 jobs) — insert/skip each job into every feasible tech/position with value pruning  
4. Output: one route per technician, ordinal ID order; lexicographic tie-break on job sequences

### Constraints
Skills, windows (wait allowed), shift return, directed asymmetric travel, single assignment, deterministic.
