**Algorithm** (`src/ReplicatedShardRebalancer/RebalanceSolver.cs`, called from `Rebalance`):
1. **Normalize** — nodes/shards sorted ordinally, zones interned, per-shard allowed pool = non-excluded nodes with `capacity >= size`; required zone diversity = `min(RF, distinct zones in pool)`.
2. **Candidate enumeration** — all RF-subsets per shard in lexicographic order, pruned by zone-coverage bounds (skipped if the pool explodes past caps).
3. **Three greedy seeds** (min resulting utilization / max slack / current-preserving) followed by **local search** (full-candidate or single-replacement reassignment, plus cross-shard replica swaps) over the objective `(overflow, maxUtil, spread, movedBytes, movedReplicas)`; overflow leads so infeasible seeds self-repair.
4. **Branch-and-bound** — DFS in sorted shard order with lower bounds on max utilization (weighted average + node budget availability), spread, moved bytes, and replicas. Pass 1 proves the optimal objective tuple; pass 2 replays in lex order so the ordinally smallest placement wins remaining ties. All limits are step-based, not time-based, so output stays deterministic.
