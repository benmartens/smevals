## Summary

Implemented `ReplicatedShardRebalancer.Rebalance` as an exact deterministic backtracking solver.

**Algorithm**
1. Sort nodes/shards by ordinal ID.
2. For each shard, enumerate eligible node combinations of size `replicationFactor` that hit required zone diversity (pre-sorted → lex order).
3. DFS assign combinations under capacity limits.
4. Lex-minimize: max utilization → utilization spread (via max min-util) → moved bytes → moved replicas.
5. Ordered DFS keeps the ordinally smallest placement on metric ties.
6. Prune on incumbent max-util / move-cost bounds; emit canonical target placements.
